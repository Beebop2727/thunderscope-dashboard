ThunderScope user-generated Bitchin' Betty TTS pack
====================================================

Thirty-five recordings in ThunderScope v0.10.1 were supplied directly by the
project user across [Bitchin' Betty]2.zip and the later Gear Overspeed upload. The MP3 originals were converted to mono
44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV, trimmed, and loudness-normalised for consistent,
low-latency playback on Windows and Linux.

The user specifically requested that the high-angle warning say "AoA" rather
than "Angle of attack".

This notice describes provenance only. The user is responsible for complying
with the terms of the TTS service used to generate the original recordings.
