Radio chatter sources and importer

Internal RIO/WSO clips remain in audio\radio\generic, takeoff, cruise, combat, landing, or ground. VAICOM recordings are imported into audio\radio\vaicom\THEME by running import_vaicom_chatter.bat on Windows or ./import_vaicom_chatter.sh on Linux. VAICOM audio is treated as external radio traffic, not as direct crew dialogue. Chatter and Betty use independent playback channels, so warnings can play over an existing transmission. Disable the overlap option only if you prefer the older interruption behaviour.
